@61000
Kanehira Tsunanori ?-1625

@61001
A Tsugaru vassal; Morizumi's heir, of the Oura kin. One of the "Three Oura Elders" who upheld Tamenobu in founding the house. The Kanehira name is said to date from Morizumi's taking Kanehira village as his fief.

@61002
Kitabatake Akimura 1555-?

@61003
Last head of the Oshu Kitabatake; married a daughter of Ando Chikasue. When the Tsugaru took his castle he fled to Ando lands and changed his name to Kitabatake Danjo. Some say he killed himself as the castle fell.

@61004
Ichikuri Takaharu ?-1614

@61005
An Osaki vassal; lord of Ichikuri Castle in Oshu. The Ichikuri are said to have been the greatest house among the Osaki vassals. After the Osaki uprising was crushed he served Mogami Yoshiaki; later rebelled and was killed.

@61006
Kiyono Naganori ?-1634

@61007
An Ashina vassal; Hirata Tsunehiro's second son. The Hirata are said to have been one of the four pillars among the Ashina elders. After the Ashina fell he served the Uesugi as an attendant and kept Ina Castle.

@61008
Sato Yoshinobu ?-?

@61009
A Soma vassal. Served the Iwaki, then went over to Soma Akitane. Served well as war magistrate, but slander cost him his lands, and he died of illness in despair.

@61010
Sato Tamenobu ?-1591

@61011
Yoshinobu's son; lord of Kosai Castle in Mutsu. Killed his father's foe Kori Samanosuke and, taking Samanosuke's heir as hostage, joined Date Masamune. Later died in the fighting at Sanuma.

@61012
Isonome Hidekane ?-?

@61013
An Ando vassal; lord of Odate Castle and a direct retainer of Chikasue. When Asari Katsuyori, ruler of Hinai, lost to the Ando army and fled, Chikasue made him deputy of Hinai.

@61014
Yagashiwa Naoie ?-?

@61015
A Mogami vassal; master of Yagashiwa Hall. When Date Harumune and Terumune fell out, he went to the Date as an envoy of peace. He was also present when Mogami Yoshiaki and Date Terumune met to be reconciled.

@61016
Koyanagawa Morimune 1523-1595

@61017
A Date vassal; Chikamune's son and lord of Takahata Castle in Dewa. Said to be a brave and shrewd adviser who led the vanguard against the Mogami. In old age he attended Masamune and offered him much advice.

@61018
Nukada Terumichi ?-1630

@61019
A Hitachi local lord; last head of the Nukada Onozaki. Fought bravely to resist his lords the Satake, but failed. Then served Date Masamune and Matsudaira Tadateru. Said to have been a fine painter.

@61020
Mizunoya Masamura 1521-1596

@61021
Harumochi's heir; a local lord of Shimodate Castle in Hitachi, serving the Yuki. Confirmed in his lands by Toyotomi Hideyoshi, he left the house to his brother Katsutoshi and retired to Shimokuda Castle in Shimotsuke.

@61022
Horie Yoritada ?-1617

@61023
Chief elder of the Satomi. When the Satomi lost their domain, he followed his lord Tadayoshi to Hoki, where he died of illness. His grave is said to be at Daigakuin temple in Kurayoshi.

@61024
Kasama Tsunaie ?-1590

@61025
Lord of Kasama Castle in Hitachi. The Kasama are said to descend from the Shioya, a local house of the Kamakura era. First served the Utsunomiya, but sided with the Hojo at Odawara and died in battle.

@61026
Oishi Tsunamoto ?-1601

@61027
A Yamanouchi-Uesugi vassal. After his lords fell he served Uesugi Kagekatsu. When Kagekatsu moved to Aizu, he became keeper of Hobara Castle and a weighty figure as one of the Three Magistrates of Aizu.

@61028
Fujita Yasukuni ?-1555

@61029
A Yamanouchi-Uesugi vassal. Surrendered to Hojo Ujiyasu's invasion of Musashi. When Ujiyasu's third son Ujikuni married Yasukuni's daughter and took over the Fujita, he moved to Yodo Castle and took the name Yodo.

@61030
Ishimaki Yasumasa 1534-1613

@61031
A Hojo vassal. Went to Kyoto to plead for his lord Hojo Ujinao, but was seized and confined by Toyotomi Hideyoshi. After the Hojo fell he served Tokugawa Ieyasu.

@61032
Kuno Muneyoshi 1527-1609

@61033
Lord of Kuno Castle. Served the Imagawa, but in league with Koriki Kiyonaga, submitted to Tokugawa Ieyasu's invasion of Totomi. Given Sakura in Shimosa when Ieyasu moved to the Kanto; later went back to Kuno Castle.

@61034
Toriyao Mitsuhide ?-?

@61035
A Kitabatake vassal who served Harutomo, Tomonori and Tomofusa. Acted as governor of the port city of Ominato in Ise. Said to have been a fine general, both wise and brave.

@61036
Hajikano Masatsugu 1545-1624

@61037
A Takeda vassal of Kai; succeeded Hajikano Gengoro, killed at Kawanakajima. After the Takeda fell he served Tokugawa Ieyasu, distinguishing himself at Komaki, Odawara, Osaka and elsewhere.

@61038
Shimazu Tadanao ?-1604

@61039
Lord of Naganuma Castle in Shinano. Robbed of his lands by Takeda Shingen, he turned to Uesugi Kenshin. After the Takeda fell, Uesugi Kagekatsu took Kawanakajima, and he could return to Naganuma Castle.

@61040
Uchigashima Ujimasa ?-1585

@61041
Lord of Kaerikumo Castle in Hida. Submitted to the invasion of Hida by Kanamori Nagachika, a Toyotomi vassal, but soon after a great earthquake buried the castle and his house perished. Legends of buried gold live on.

@61042
Iwai Nobuyoshi ?-1620

@61043
An Uesugi vassal. For his merit in the Otate Rebellion he was made lord of Iiyama Castle in Shinano. Ordered to repair it, he laid out the castle town, said to be the basis of the later town of Iiyama.

@61044
Terasaki Morinaga ?-1581

@61045
A Jinbo vassal; lord of Gankaiji Castle in Etchu. Won merit, killing Iida Toshitada, lord of Ida Castle, among others. Then served Uesugi Kenshin, but submitted to Oda Nobunaga's invasion of Etchu and was made to kill himself.

@61046
Henmi Masatsune ?-1581

@61047
A local lord of Takahama Castle in Wakasa. The Henmi, a branch of the Wakasa Takeda, were their rivals and fought Takeda Yoshizumi. Later he submitted to Oda Nobunaga's invasion of Wakasa and kept his old lands.

@61048
Keya Takehisa 1554-1628

@61049
Born in Omi. Served the Rokkaku, Shibata, Maeda, Ikeda and Sassa in turn, then the Kuroda. An excellent scout who won much merit.

@61050
Ninagawa Chikanaga 1534-1610

@61051
Lord of Bankonji Castle in Tanba; an expert in court customs under the Ashikaga Shoguns. Later served the Chosokabe, then the Tokugawa; skilled at checking supplies and quelling uprisings, he became a storyteller.

@61052
Araki Ujitsuna ?-?

@61053
Lord of Saikusho Castle in Tanba; a valiant warrior who shone in many battles. After his lords the Hatano fell, he refused Akechi Mitsuhide's offer of service, sending his son Ujikiyo instead.

@61054
Ibaraki Nagataka ?-?

@61055
Deputy of Hosokawa Harumoto. Killed Hosokawa Takakuni, allied with Miyoshi Masanaga to destroy Miyoshi Motonaga, and crushed the Hokke uprising in Kyoto. His fate after Masanaga's defeat at Eguchi in Settsu is unknown.

@61056
Shichijo Kanenaka ?-1582

@61057
Lord of Shichijo Castle in Awa; died in defeat at Nakatomigawa. A warrior of great strength, the subject of many legends. Kanenaka's spirit is still enshrined at the Matsushima Shrine today.

@61058
Hayuka Suketoshi ?-1582

@61059
Lord of Hayuka Castle in Sanuki. When attacked by the Chosokabe army, he gave up the castle with his second son as hostage and kept his lands. Later died in battle attacking Sogo Castle.

@61060
Ido Yoshihiro 1534-1612

@61061
A Tsutsui vassal. Held Ido Castle and defended it well against Matsunaga Hisahide. Later served Oda Nobunaga, but sided with Akechi Mitsuhide at Honnoji and was stripped of his domain.

@61062
Oka Yoshimasa ?-?

@61063
A warrior of the Saika band of Kii. Joined the Saika uprising at first but soon submitted to Oda Nobunaga. Later he submitted to Hideyoshi too, when Toyotomi Hideyoshi attacked Kii.

@61064
Morishita Michiyo ?-1581

@61065
A senior vassal of the Inaba Yamana. Later joined the Mori and held Tottori Castle under Kikkawa Tsuneie. When Hashiba Hideyoshi took Tottori Castle, he killed himself with Tsuneie.

@61066
Misawa Tamekiyo 1536-1588

@61067
Lord of Misawa Castle in Izumo. His father Tameyuki died in a defeat by the Ouchi, and he joined them. During the Ouchi invasion of Izumo he briefly went back to the Amago, but submitted to the Mori army sent against them.

@61068
Saha Takahide ?-?

@61069
Lord of Hachiman Castle in Iwami. Served the Ouchi; when they fell, submitted to the Mori. During Mori Terumoto's Korean campaign he kept Hiroshima Castle.

@61070
Fukuhara Sukenari ?-1577

@61071
Lord of Takakurayama Castle in Harima; brother-in-law of Akamatsu Norinao. Attacked by Hashiba Hideyoshi on Oda Nobunaga's orders, he died in defeat as his castle fell with the other castles of Sayo.

@61072
Harada Sadasuke ?-1586

@61073
An Ukita vassal; Tadanaga's son. Lost his seat Inariyama Castle to Yamana Ujikane and lay low for a time, but later recovered it with Ukita Naoie's backing.

@61074
Nobuhara Kageyoshi ?-?

@61075
An Uragami vassal who joined the Ukita after the Uragami fell. Based at Kamiyama Castle in Mimasaka, he worked to hunt down the Uragami remnants. Said to have been both wise and brave.

@61076
Ueno Takanori ?-1575

@61077
Son-in-law of Mimura Motochika. After Motochika killed himself, he held out in Tsuneyama Castle against the Mori, but at the end of his strength killed himself. All the women and children, his wife among them, died with him.

@61078
Minakata Narimasa ?-?

@61079
A Mori vassal; lord of Migitadake Castle in Suo. Ordered by Mori Motonari to put down Ouchi Teruhiro's revolt, he made ready at Migitadake. Then attacked Teruhiro as he retreated and killed him.

@61080
Niho Takayasu ?-?

@61081
An Ouchi vassal; a magistrate under Yoshitaka, then served Yoshinaga after Yoshitaka's death. After the Ouchi fell he served the Mori, and Motonari posted him to guard Moji Castle in Buzen.

@61082
Kaneko Motoie ?-1585

@61083
A local lord of Iyo; lord of Kaneko Castle in Iyo. Aided Chosokabe Motochika in unifying Shikoku; praised as a brave general of fine character and judgement. Beaten by Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Shikoku army, he killed himself.

@61084
Satake Chikanao ?-1615

@61085
A Chosokabe vassal; lord of Kaminokae Castle in Tosa, married to a daughter of Chosokabe Motochika. In the Osaka campaigns he followed Morichika into Osaka Castle and died in the battle of Yao.

@61086
Sada Takaoki ?-?

@61087
Of a branch of the Utsunomiya; leader of the Usa district band of Buzen. Served the Ouchi, then the Otomo after their fall. When Ajimu Rinsei rebelled, he attacked him and talked him into submission.

@61088
Munakata Ujisada 1537-1586

@61089
Masauji's son; chief priest of the Munakata Shrine. Built the largest domain the Munakata ever held, but died suddenly without an heir; in Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Kyushu campaign all their lands were seized and the house ended.

@61090
Tajiri Akitane ?-?

@61091
A Ryuzoji vassal; lord of Takao Castle in Chikugo. Won merit, but was suspected of dealing secretly with the Shimazu. Through Nabeshima Nobumasa's mediation he then wrote a sworn oath of loyalty.

@61092
Samuta Nagakata 1546-1578

@61093
Lord of Okobata Castle in Higo. Said to have always hunted at the border with his dogs. Refused the Shimazu's overtures and was murdered in an ambush on a hunting trip.

@61094
Ito Sukeyasu ?-1572

@61095
Yoshisuke's brother. At Kizakibaru, an army led by Shimazu Yoshihiro and others, a tenth the size of his own, beat him with confusing tactics and surprise attacks; he died in battle.

@61096
Ijichi Shigeoki 1528-1580

@61097
A local lord of Osumi. Resisted the Shimazu as a Kimotsuki ally, but when Shimazu Toshihisa took his seat, Obama Castle, he surrendered. From then on followed the Shimazu, fighting against the Otomo and others.

@61098
Tanegashima Hisatoki 1568-1611

@61099
A Shimazu vassal; Tokitaka's second son. Elder to Shimazu Yoshihiro and Iehisa, he won fame with firearms. During Toyotomi Hideyoshi's attack on Odawara he presented Hideyoshi with 200 guns.
