Father of Hojo Masako, Yoritomo's wife. At Yoritomo's side from the Minamoto uprising on, he helped defeat the Taira. On Yoritomo's death he ousted the heir, became regent and seized power.

Second Khan of the Mongol Empire, Genghis Khan's third son. Relied on Yelu Chucai to order the government. Sent armies against the Jin and into Europe to expand his realm.
