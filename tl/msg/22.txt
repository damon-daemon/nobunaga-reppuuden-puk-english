@22000
Hashiba Hidetsugu 1568-1595

@22001
Nephew and adopted son of Toyotomi Hideyoshi; became Regent. After Hideyori's birth his excesses earned him the name "Murderous Regent"; forced to commit suicide for treason.

@22002
Ishikawa Goemon 1558-1594

@22003
Iga ninja, said to be Momochi Sandayu's lieutenant. Stole into Osaka Castle to kill Toyotomi Hideyoshi, but the famed "Plover Censer" cried out. Said to have been boiled alive.

@22004
Hisatake Chikanao ?-?

@22005
Chosokabe vassal. His plots and slanders drove many colleagues to suicide, causing his clan's fall. His brother Chikanobu said: "He will surely bring ruin on the Chosokabe."

@22006
Miyoshi Masakatsu 1536-1631

@22007
Son of Miyoshi Masanaga. Fought Miyoshi Chokei, who drove his father to his death; after Chokei died, made peace with the Miyoshi Triumvirate. Later served Oda Nobunaga, then the Toyotomi and Tokugawa.

@22008
Ichijo Kanesada 1543-1585

@22009
Lord of Nakamura Castle, Tosa. Lived for wine and women day and night; his disgusted retainers drove him out. With Otomo Sorin's backing he tried to regain his lands, but failed.

@22010
Kono Michinao 1564-1587

@22011
Lord of Yuzuki Castle, Iyo. Submitted to the invading Chosokabe Motochika; fought Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Shikoku campaign and lost. Stripped of his lands, he moved to Aki.

@22012
Kurushima Michiyasu 1519-1567

@22013
Kono vassal; head of the Kurushima Murakami navy. Named heir by his lord Michinao, but the vassals objected. At Itsukushima he aided the Mori and helped them win.

@22014
Matsudaira Tadayoshi 1580-1607

@22015
Fourth son of Tokugawa Ieyasu; full brother of Hidetada, the 2nd Edo shogun. A gifted warrior; at Sekigahara he fought Shimazu Yoshihiro's army and won honour despite a wound.

@22016
Aso Koremasa ?-1583

@22017
Became high priest of Aso on his father Koretoyo's death. Kept good ties with the Otomo while siding with the Shimazu or Ryuzoji as needed; his deft diplomacy held his domain.

@22018
Matsudaira Tadateru 1592-1683

@22019
Sixth son of Tokugawa Ieyasu; shunned by his father for his ugliness. Later held Takada in Echigo (750,000 koku); his late arrival at Osaka raised suspicion of Toyotomi ties and he was attainted.

@22020
Yagyu Muneyoshi 1527-1606

@22021
Yamato landholder. Mastered the secrets under Kamiizumi Nobutsuna and founded the Yagyu Shinkage school. Taught it to Tokugawa Ieyasu; the Yagyu became the Tokugawa's sword instructors.

@22022
Matsura Shigenobu 1549-1614

@22023
Son of Takanobu; became lord of Hirado Castle, Hizen, when his father retired. Licensed by Toyotomi Hideyoshi for foreign trade; hosted Dutch and English trading posts and made Hirado thrive.

@22024
Sagara Yoshihi 1544-1581

@22025
Heir of Haruhiro; succeeded under the guardianship of his grandfather Uemura Yorioki. Survived two revolts and grew his domain. Killed fighting the Aso as a Shimazu vassal.

@22026
Sagara Yorifusa 1574-1636

@22027
Second son of Yoshihi; succeeded when his brother Tadafusa died young. Aided Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Kyushu campaign; held Hitoyoshi, Higo (20,000 koku). Kept it by siding with the East at Sekigahara.

@22028
Fukami Nagatomo ?-1590

@22029
Sagara vassal, gifted in diplomacy. A senior retainer who upheld the clan with Indo Yoriyasu; went as envoy to the Shimazu and Toyotomi and worked hard for the clan's survival.

@22030
Indo Yoriyasu 1521-1606

@22031
Sagara vassal, renowned for valour; fought at Minamata Castle, Higo, and elsewhere. When his lord Yoshihi fell, he and Fukami Nagatomo guarded the boy lord Yorifusa and saved the clan.

@22032
Ito Yoshisuke 1512-1585

@22033
Lord of Tonokori Castle, Hyuga; built the Ito's largest domain. Beaten by the Shimazu, he fled to Otomo Sorin in Bungo; when the Otomo lost at Mimikawa, he fled on to Iyo.

@22034
Ito Suketaka 1559-1600

@22035
Son of Yoshisuke. Guided Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Kyushu campaign and regained his old lands at Obi, Hyuga (36,000 koku). Sided with the East at Sekigahara; died of illness soon after.

@22036
Inatomi Sukenao 1552-1611

@22037
Isshiki vassal; founder of the Inatomi school of gunnery. Served Hosokawa Tadaoki as gunnery master, later the Tokugawa; as shogunal gun officer he organised the Kunitomo smiths.

@22038
Shimazu Iehisa 1547-1587

@22039
Fourth son of Takahisa. At Okitanawate he beat 60,000 Ryuzoji troops with 3,000. Submitted to Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Kyushu campaign; died suddenly just after meeting Toyotomi Hidenaga.

@22040
Tanegashima Tokitaka 1528-1579

@22041
Shimazu vassal, lord of Tanegashima. Bought 2 guns from Portuguese traders cast ashore there, studied and improved them, and made the first Japanese "Tanegashima" guns.

@22042
Mori Tahee 1556-1615

@22043
Kuroda vassal; with Goto Matabee, one of the clan's two fiercest warriors. Drained a huge cup filled by Fukushima Masanori and won the spear "Nihongo"; the song "Kuroda-bushi" recalls it.

@22044
Ijuin Tadaaki ?-?

@22045
Shimazu vassal. Served Takahisa in uniting Satsuma; at the siege of Kajiki Castle, Osumi, first used guns in battle in Japan. Later became an elder and shaped Shimazu government.

@22046
Ijuin Tadamune ?-1599

@22047
Son of Tadaao; Shimazu vassal. Became a hostage when Toyotomi Hideyoshi beat the clan; was given lands on a par with his lord's, but Tadatsune suspected him and cut him down.

@22048
Niiro Tadamoto 1526-1610

@22049
Shimazu vassal; a fierce general called "Demon Musashi", famed in many battles. Wrote rules for training young vassals that became a model and raised many able retainers.

@22050
Kyogoku Takatsugu 1563-1609

@22051
Eldest son of Takayoshi. His sister was Toyotomi Hideyoshi's concubine and his wife Lady Yodo's sister, so Hideyoshi favoured him. Held Otsu Castle for the East; then fled to Mt. Koya.

@22052
Hanabusa Motohide 1549-1616

@22053
Ukita vassal; a brave man, often first to strike or first over the wall. Cursed Toyotomi Hideyoshi for watching Noh at Odawara, but was praised for his daring.

@22054
Yagyu Munenori 1571-1646

@22055
Shinkage swordsman. With his father Muneyoshi he served the Tokugawa as fencing master to Hidetada and Iemitsu. Trusted by Iemitsu, he became chief inspector and a daimyo of 10,000 koku.

@22056
Momochi Sandayu ?-?

@22057
Iga landholder based at Ueno; head of one of the three great Iga ninja houses. Repelled Oda Nobukatsu's army at Kibu Fort and resisted the Oda to the very end.

@22058
Ouchi Yoshinaga 1532-1557

@22059
Second son of Otomo Yoshiaki. After Ouchi Yoshitaka's death, Sue Harukata made him head of the Ouchi. When Harukata fell at Itsukushima, he could not stop the Mori; fled to Nagato and died by his own hand.

@22060
Otomo Yoshimune 1558-1610

@22061
Heir of Sorin. Pressed by the Shimazu and Ryuzoji, he turned to Toyotomi Hideyoshi and was confirmed in Bungo. Attainted for fleeing the enemy in the Korean campaign.

@22062
Iwaki Shigetaka ?-1569

@22063
Lord of Odate Castle, Mutsu, and a fine poet. Often fought Satake Yoshiatsu; ruled from Mutsu into part of Hitachi. Went to Kyoto and received court rank and office.

@22064
Akai Naomasa 1529-1578

@22065
Lord of Kuroi Castle, Tanba; a valiant man called "the Red Demon of Tanba". Repelled Akechi Mitsuhide's invasion; later slain by Wakizaka Yasuharu, a vassal of Hashiba Hideyoshi.

@22066
Honganji Shonyo 1516-1554

@22067
10th abbot of the Honganji. When Nichiren followers and Rokkaku Sadayori burned the Yamashina Honganji, he moved to Ishiyama; aided by kin and vassals, he steadily grew in power.

@22068
Amago Kunihisa 1492-1554

@22069
Amago vassal. Led the Shingu band in support of his nephew Haruhisa, but they fell out and Haruhisa killed him. The cause was a plot by Mori Motonari to weaken the Amago.

@22070
Amago Katsuhisa 1553-1578

@22071
Son of Masahisa. Raised by Yamanaka Shikanosuke and others to restore the Amago; with Oda Nobunaga's help he tried to retake Izumo but failed. Beaten by the Mori at Kozuki, Harima; took his life.

@22072
Satomi Yoshiyori 1543-1587

@22073
Made Umeomaru, son of his brother Yoshihiro, a monk and took the headship. When Masaki Noritoki of Otaki, Kazusa, rebelled in league with the Hojo, he crushed him and pacified Kazusa.

@22074
Yoshida Takayori ?-?

@22075
Chosokabe vassal, married to Kunichika's sister. Known for both wit and valour; used many stratagems and won fame in battle. As Kunichika's right hand he helped the clan expand.

@22076
Ryuzoji Masaie 1566-1607

@22077
Heir of Takanobu. Led the vanguard in Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Kyushu campaign; confirmed in 7 districts of Hizen. Too ill to serve, he left rule to his vassal Nabeshima Naoshige with Hideyoshi's leave.

@22078
Suzuki Shigenori 1547-1589

@22079
Sanada vassal, keeper of Nagurumi Castle, Kozuke. Surprised and beaten by Hojo vassal Inomata Kuninori; took his own life. The breach of the ban on private war gave Toyotomi Hideyoshi a pretext for Odawara.

@22080
Ashikaga Yoshiuji 1541-1583

@22081
Son of Haruuji. Raised by the Hojo, he returned to Koga Castle, Shimosa, but had no real power. He left no son, so the Koga Ashikaga died out; his daughter founded the Kitsuregawa house.

@22082
Chiba Tanetomi 1525-1579

@22083
Lord of Sakura Castle, Shimosa. Submitted to the Hojo against the Satake pushing south and the Satomi pushing north. Repelled an attack by the Uesugi and Satomi.

@22084
Shiba Yoshimune 1513-1554

@22085
Constable of Owari; a puppet of deputy constable Oda Nobutomo at Kiyosu Castle. When Nobutomo plotted to kill Oda Nobunaga, he warned Nobunaga; Nobutomo had him murdered.

@22086
Sagara Haruhiro 1513-1555

@22087
Lord of Furufumoto Castle, Higo; son of Uemura Yorioki. Adopted heir of Sagara Yoshishige. Traded with Ming China and issued the Sagara house laws to strengthen his rule.

@22088
Togo Shigetada 1561-1643

@22089
Shimazu vassal. While in Kyoto with his lord Yoshihisa, learned swordsmanship from Zenkichi of Tenneiji. Founded the Jigen school and taught the sword to Tadatsune.

@22090
Takeda Nobukado 1528-1582

@22091
Shingen's younger brother. After his brother Nobushige fell, led the kinsmen and aided Shingen. Looked like Shingen and served as his double. A gifted painter; left portraits and other works.

@22092
Takeda Yoshinobu 1538-1567

@22093
Eldest son of Shingen; his wife was Imagawa Yoshimoto's daughter. Fought at Kawanakajima. Opposed the attack on the Imagawa and plotted revolt but failed; died in confinement, by suicide or illness.

@22094
Anegakoji Yoshiyori ?-1572

@22095
Hida landholder. Took over the extinct Anegakoji house, governors of Hida, changing his name from Mitsugi. Waged war and diplomacy against the Ema, who were tied to the Takeda.

@22096
Kitajo Takahiro ?-?

@22097
Uesugi vassal; brave but rash. Lured by Takeda Shingen, Hojo Ujiyasu and others, he rebelled several times, but each time Kenshin pardoned him and took him back.

@22098
Asakura Kagetake ?-1575

@22099
Asakura kinsman; fought hard as Asakura commander-in-chief at Anegawa. After his clan's fall he served Oda Nobunaga, but surrendered to the Ikko; angering Nobunaga, he had to kill himself.
