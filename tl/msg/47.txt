@47000
Victory and Defeat 1

@47001
You win when you meet one of these conditions.\n\n[Unification by Force]\nRule all 40 main castles in Japan.\n\n[Unification by Alliance]\nBecome Shogun, hold at least the set number of main castles (Beginner 15, Advanced 20), and be allied with every other clan.

@47002
Victory and Defeat 2

@47003
You lose when one of these happens.\n\n[Destruction]\nYour daimyo dies with no heir, or you lose every main castle.\n[Another Clan Unifies]\nAnother clan achieves unification by alliance.\n[Time Up]\nYou have not won by 1700.

@47004
Prestige 1

@47005
A daimyo's might and standing. It affects diplomacy and officers' loyalty. High Prestige brings these benefits:\n\n[Benefits of Prestige]\n- [Intrigue] and [Diplomacy] succeed more easily.\n- Officers are more loyal.\n- Other clans' officers and independent powers surrender more easily.

@47006
Prestige 2

@47007
Prestige is the sum of castles ruled, fame, posts, court titles, marriages, rule of Kyoto, and court relations.\n\n[Raising Prestige]\n- Take castles.\n- Win battles.\n- Receive high posts or court titles.\n- Marry into other clans.\n- Rule Nijo Castle or the Muromachi Palace.

@47008
Ranks and Troops 1

@47009
Every officer has a rank. There are 6 ranks. An officer rises in rank as he gains merit; merit is gained by carrying out commands. The higher the rank, the more troops he can lead in battle.

@47010
Ranks and Troops 2

@47011
Each rank's troop limit and the merit needed to reach it:\n\nRank: troops / merit\nCaptain: 500 / -\nGeneral: 600 / 100\nCommander: 700 / 300\nElder: 800 / 600\nSenior Elder: 900 / 999\nDaimyo: 1000 / -

@47012
Officer Traits 1

@47013
Officers have a personality, skills and a calling.\n\n[Personality]\nAffects loyalty and how well officers get on. By how much they value Prestige, they are "Ignores", "Low", "High" or "Absolute".

@47014
Officer Traits 2

@47015
[Skills]\nAffect related commands and ways of attacking. There are domestic skills and battle skills.\n\n- Domestic: Farming, Commerce,\n  Building, Diplomacy,\n  Recruit\n- Battle: Volley, Fire Pots,\n  Cav. Charge, Mtd. Gunners

@47016
Officer Traits 3

@47017
[Calling]\nAffects related commands. The callings are ninja, swordsman, tea master and monk.

@47018
Castles and Domains

@47019
There are main castles and branch castles. Each has a domain, and its development terrain brings in gold and rice regularly. Domain sizes:\n\n[Main castle] 5 tiles around it\n[Branch castle] 3 tiles around it

@47020
Development Terrain 1

@47021
Built in a domain, it brings in gold, rice and so on. Paddies, towns and villages have levels 1 to 3: the higher the level, the larger the income.

@47022
Development Terrain 2

@47023
Terrain types and their income:\n\nPaddy (LV1-3): rice (9th month)\nField: rice (9th month)\nTown (LV1-3): gold (monthly)\nSpeciality: rice or gold\nVillage (LV1-3): troops (monthly)\nFree Market: troops (monthly)\nGunsmith Village: guns (monthly)

@47024
Domestic Plan

@47025
When you carry out [Develop], the plan says which tiles of the domain get which development terrain, and in what order. One development unit can be given several jobs at once, and then develops the land by itself, following the plan.

@47026
Unit Types 1

@47027
The kind of troops in a unit. In battle, one officer plus his troops make one unit. The unit type decides attack, defence, movement and ways of attacking.

@47028

Gunners: 10/5/18, MF, Charge, Guns\nGunners: 2/3/8, Guns, Cannon

@47030
Unit Types 3

@47031

@47032
Corps Size

@47033
Set by the number of units in a corps unit. The corps size decides the size of the battle.\n\n[Small] 5 units or fewer\n[Medium] 6 to 10 units\n[Large] 11 units or more

@47034
Small Field Battle 1

@47035
A skirmish between small forces. With no schemes or formations, strong units have the edge. Attacking or protecting the commander-in-chief decides the battle.\n\n[Occurs when]\nA small corps unit attacks a corps unit.

@47036
Small Field Battle 2

@47037
Battle commands in a small field battle (* = C-in-C only):\n\nMove\nAttack: Normal, Charge, Bows, Guns, Joint\nRider: Sortie*\nArms: Foot, Pikes, Bows\nRetire

@47038
Medium Field Battle 1

@47039
A typical field battle between daimyo, like Okehazama or Kawanakajima. The C-in-C has a formation; a strategist appears, and both can use [Ruse]. [Formation] attacks by the units in a formation are the key.\n\n[Occurs when]\nA medium corps unit attacks a corps unit.

@47044
Large Field Battle 1

@47045
A great pitched battle like Nagashino. Only units inside the C-in-C's command range can act. A supply unit and deputies appear; deputies have formations and can act outside the range. Chains of [Formation] attacks decide the battle.\n[Occurs when]\nA large corps unit attacks a corps unit.

@47050
Medium Siege 1

@47051
Like the siege of Tottori. The goal is to take or hold the inner keep. The gate must be broken to get in, so the fight at the gate decides it. Attackers do well to break it with strong, large units; defenders, to mass bows and guns.\n[Occurs when]\nThe castle has 5 towers at most, and both corps are medium or smaller.

Large Siege 1

@47057

@47062
Naval Battle

@47063
Like the battle of Kizugawaguchi. Comes in large, medium and small. Unit types depend on rank and ironclads. Same rules as a field battle, except no [Ploy], and ironclad units can use [Attack]-[Cannon]. Ironclads, strong in attack and defence, decide the battle.\n[Occurs when]\nA corps unit attacks one at sea, on a lake or in port.

@47064
Ending a Battle

@47065
You win the battle when you meet one of these conditions. If 30 turns pass with no result, it is a draw.\n- Kill the enemy C-in-C.\n- Capture the enemy C-in-C.\n- Make the enemy C-in-C retreat.\n- Bring the enemy corps' morale to 0.\n- Take the inner keep (siege).\n- Make them accept a demand (siege).
