@28000
Miyoshi Seikai ?-?

@28001
One of the Ten Sanada Braves; fictional, said to be modelled on Miyoshi Masayasu. Wielded an iron staff 8 shaku long. When Osaka fell, saw Toyotomi Hideyori and others escape, then took his own life.

@28002
Miyoshi Isa ?-?

@28003
One of the Ten Sanada Braves; fictional. A master of the staff who fought hard in the Osaka campaigns. When Osaka fell, he killed himself with his elder brother Seikai, reciting comic verse.

@28004
Kimotsuki Yoshikane 1535-1571

@28005
Kanetsugu's heir. Allied with the Ito and took Obi Castle in Hyuga. When Shimazu Yoshihisa attacked Obama Castle in Osumi, he marched to relieve Ijichi Shigeoki and drove off the Shimazu army.

@28006
Yuri Kamanosuke ?-?

@28007
One of the Ten Sanada Braves; fictional. Tried to stop the Sanada army's march to Shizugatake, but lost a duel to Anayama Kosuke and was captured. Said to have served the Sanada from then on.

@28008
Kimotsuki Kanesuke ?-1573

@28009
Kanetsugu's second son. Succeeded his brother Yoshikane and opposed the Shimazu, allied with the Ito and Osumi clans. Ousted by a plot of his mother (a daughter of Shimazu Tadayoshi) and his vassals.

@28010
Kimotsuki Kanemori 1561-1600

@28011
Kanetsugu's third son; became head when his brother Kanesuke was ousted. Isolated as the Osumi clans went over to the Shimazu, he gave up his lands and submitted. Killed at Sekigahara.

@28012
Anayama Kosuke ?-?

@28013
One of the Ten Sanada Braves; fictional. Same age as his lord Yukimura and much like him in looks. Served as Yukimura's double in the Osaka campaigns and charged the Tokugawa army; said to have died fighting.

@28014
Shimazu Toyohisa 1570-1600

@28015
Son of Iehisa (Takahisa's fourth son). Won merit in the Korean campaign and elsewhere. At Sekigahara he led the rearguard of the retreat and died after a fierce fight with Honda Tadakatsu, Ii Naomasa and others.

@28016
Ijuin Tadaao ?-?

@28017
Tadaaki's son. With his father he defeated Kimotsuki Kanehiro at Kajiki Castle in Osumi and settled his lands. Helped Takahisa unify Satsuma; later became a senior councillor.

@28018
Kakei Juzo ?-?

@28019
One of the Ten Sanada Braves; fictional, said to be modelled on Kakei Jubei or Kakei Kinrokuro, real warriors under Yukimura. Said to have distinguished himself in the Osaka campaigns.

@28020
Nezu Jinpachi ?-?

@28021
One of the Ten Sanada Braves; fictional, said to be modelled on Nezu Koroku, a hereditary Sanada vassal. In the summer siege of Osaka he served as Yukimura's double with Anayama Kosuke and died in battle.

@28022
Unno Rokuro ?-?

@28023
One of the Ten Sanada Braves; fictional, said to be modelled on the real vassals Unno Koheita or Unno Rokurobei. Sanada Yukitaka is said to have been a younger brother of the 29th head of the Unno.

@28024
Mochizuki Rokuro ?-?

@28025
One of the Ten Sanada Braves; fictional, said to be modelled on Mochizuki Uemon, who followed Yukimura into Osaka Castle. Said to come from the Mochizuki, one of the three noble Shigeno houses of Shinano.

@28026
Murakami Kunikiyo 1546-?

@28027
Yoshikiyo's son. When Takeda Shingen took their seat in Shinano, he fled to Echigo with his father. Served the Uesugi, later taking the name Yamaura; helped conclude peace with Tokugawa Ieyasu.

@28028
Takanashi Masayori ?-?

@28029
A Shinano local lord. First under Murakami Yoshikiyo; after Yoshikiyo's fall he turned to Uesugi Kenshin. Fought in the Uesugi vanguard at Kawanakajima, but never recovered his old lands.

@28030
Yashiro Masakuni ?-?

@28031
Lord of Yashiro Castle in Shinano. First under Murakami Yoshikiyo; joined Takeda Shingen when he invaded Shinano. After the Takeda fell, followed Mori Nagayoshi, Oda vassal and lord of Kaizu Castle.

@28032
Yashiro Katsunaga 1558-1623

@28033
Masakuni's son; a Takeda vassal. After the Takeda fell he briefly joined the Uesugi, then helped Tokugawa Ieyasu pacify Shinano and served the Tokugawa. Distinguished himself in the Osaka campaigns.

@28034
Suda Mitsuchika ?-?

@28035
A Shinano local lord who led the Ikko uprisings. Later served Uesugi Kagekatsu, keeping down the Ikko in Hokuriku. As lord of Kaizu Castle in Shinano he fought Tokugawa Ieyasu's army in north Shinano.

@28036
Suda Nagayoshi 1579-1615

@28037
Mitsuchika's son; an Uesugi vassal. During Sekigahara he and Honjo Shigenaga routed Date Masamune's army at Fukushima. Won merit again at Osaka and received a commendation from Tokugawa Hidetada.

@28038
Ashida Nobushige 1548-1583

@28039
A Takeda vassal. As lord of Futamata Castle in Totomi he repulsed the Tokugawa. After the Takeda fell he served the Tokugawa in the Shinano campaign; shot while besieging Iwao Castle, he died.

@28040
Ogasawara Nagatoki 1519-1583

@28041
Lord of Fukashi Castle in Shinano; Nagamune's eldest son. Driven out by Takeda Shingen, he wandered; stayed with the Nagao, then at Akutagawa Castle in Settsu. Beaten by the Oda, he turned to the Uesugi again.

@28042
Ogasawara Hidemasa 1569-1615

@28043
Sadayoshi's heir. Served the Tokugawa; held Matsumoto in Shinano, 60,000 koku. Mortally wounded at Osaka. When Ieyasu came to see him, he said "Shinano is..." and died.

@28044
Inukai Masanori ?-?

@28045
Elder of Ogasawara Nagatoki. When Shingen drove out his lord and took the main castle, he held out at Inukai Castle. While going out to meet Nagatoki, he lost Inukai Castle and fled.

@28046
Inukai Hisatomo ?-?

@28047
An Ogasawara vassal of Shinano; Masanori's third son. Served Sadayoshi, Hidemasa and Tadazane in turn. Fought in many battles; as chief elder he held the largest fief in the house.

@28048
Futatsugi Shigeyoshi ?-?

@28049
An Ogasawara vassal of Shinano; Shigetaka's heir. Served Nagatoki, Sadayoshi and Hidemasa. With Tokugawa Ieyasu's help he drove Ogasawara Sadatane, an Uesugi ally, from Fukashi and restored Sadayoshi.

@28050
Mizoguchi Sadayasu ?-?

@28051
An Ogasawara vassal of Shinano. Served Sadayoshi as a senior retainer and worked for his return to Fukashi. Wrote the "Mizoguchi Family Record" and presented it to Ogasawara Hidemasa.

@28052
Kiso Yoshitoshi 1577-1640

@28053
Yoshimasa's son; succeeded on his father's death. Often coarse in his conduct; stripped of his domain for killing his uncle Yoshitoyo. His mother Mari later tried to restore the Kiso, in vain.

@28054
Suwa Yoritada 1536-1605

@28055
Yorishige's cousin. Under Takeda Shingen he became high priest of the Suwa Grand Shrine. After the Takeda fell he took the Suwa lands for himself; later served Tokugawa Ieyasu, who confirmed his fief.

@28056
Hoshina Masatoshi 1509-1593

@28057
A Takeda vassal; guarded Takato Castle as a leader of the Shinano vanguard. A master spearman known as "Spear Danjo". After the Takeda fell he became a vassal of Tokugawa Ieyasu.

@28058
Mitsuki Akitsuna ?-1583

@28059
Second son of Anegakoji Yoshiyori. Poisoned his adoptive father Nabeyama Yasutoshi, banished his adoptive mother and seized Nabeyama Castle in Hida. Killed with his wife for plotting against his brother Yoritsuna.

@28060
Mitsuki Kunitsuna ?-1585

@28061
Lord of Yamashita Castle in Hida, once a Shinto priest; Yoritsuna's brother-in-law. Fled from Kanamori Nagachika's Oda army, was caught and spared. Rebelled again and was killed in the attack.

@28062
Nagao Harukage 1509-1553

@28063
Tamekage's heir; succeeded his father as lord of Kasugayama Castle. Sickly and a weak leader, he faced revolt from his generals and, through Uesugi mediation, handed the house to his brother Kagetora.

@28064
Uesugi Kagenobu ?-1578

@28065
Took the Uesugi name with Kenshin when Kenshin became Kanto Kanrei. A pillar of the kin, he fought in many places. In the civil war after Kenshin's death he sided with Uesugi Kagetora and fell.

@28066
Irobe Katsunaga ?-1568

@28067
An Uesugi vassal of Echigo; lord of Hirabayashi Castle, son of Norinaga. A veteran since the time of Nagao Tamekage. Won merit at Kawanakajima in 1561 and a commendation from Uesugi Kenshin.

@28068
Irobe Akinaga ?-1587

@28069
An Uesugi vassal of Echigo; lord of Hirabayashi Castle, son of Katsunaga. Said to have received the name Akinaga from Kenshin. Fought well against Honjo Shigenaga; later left the house to his brother Nagazane.

@28070
Irobe Nagazane ?-1592

@28071
An Uesugi vassal of Echigo; lord of Hirabayashi Castle, Akinaga's brother. On Toyotomi Hideyoshi's orders he helped put down the Senboku uprising in Dewa. Went to Korea too, but fell ill and died.

@28072
Kawada Nagachika ?-1581

@28073
An Uesugi vassal; Kenshin saw his talent in Kyoto and took him into service. Did much in Kenshin's Hokuriku campaigns. After Kenshin's death he aided Kagekatsu, fighting Oda Nobunaga's army in Etchu.

@28074
Kitajo Kagehiro 1548-1579

@28075
Takahiro's son. Served the Uesugi at Umayabashi Castle in Kozuke, helping Kenshin rule the Kanto. In the Otate Rebellion he sided with Uesugi Kagetora and was assassinated by Uesugi Kagekatsu.

@28076
Honjo Norihide ?-?

@28077
An Uesugi vassal of Echigo; lord of Tochio Castle, also called Sanenori. Served at Kenshin's side with Okuma Tomohide, Naoe Sanetsuna and others; said to have won merit in Kenshin's first battle.

@28078
Yasuda Yoshimoto ?-1622

@28079
An Uesugi vassal of Echigo, born a Mori; one of the "Three Magistrates of Aizu". Fought in many battles from Kawanakajima on. Led the Uesugi vanguard against Tokugawa Ieyasu's attack on Aizu.

@28080
Amakasu Kagetsugu ?-1611

@28081
An Uesugi vassal of Echigo under Kagekatsu; a brave general ranked with Honjo Shigenaga. Took the Abe's Iwaide hall. When Kagekatsu was moved to Aizu, he followed and became keeper of Shiroishi Castle.

@28082
Onikojima Yataro 1522-1588

@28083
An Uesugi vassal of Echigo, real name Kojima Sadaoki; a hero of many legends. Served Kenshin from boyhood and won many honours. As "Demon Kojima" he struck fear into the nearby provinces.

@28084
Suibara Chikanori 1546-1616

@28085
An Uesugi vassal. Routed the Toyotomi side at Osaka and got a commendation from Tokugawa Hidetada. Said to have mocked Hidetada: "A commendation for a fight like children throwing stones..."

@28086
Jinbo Nagaki ?-?

@28087
Nagamoto's son; of a house serving the Hatakeyama since the Kamakura era. Under his father they fled from Uesugi Kenshin, then submitted to him. His father handed him the headship.

@28088
Jinbo Nagasumi ?-?

@28089
Nagamoto's son. Banished by his father, he lived as a ronin in Kyoto, then served Oda Nobunaga in the Etchu campaign. Attacked by Kojima Motoshige and others, he was confined in Toyama Castle and fell.

@28090
Jinbo Tadahiro ?-?

@28091
Kin of Nagamoto, though the link is unknown. When the Ikko rose in Etchu, his seat Hinomiya Castle was besieged; he made peace, gave up the castle and fled to Mt. Sekidozan.

@28092
Jinbo Ujiharu 1528-1592

@28093
Lord of Moriyama Castle in Etchu. Submitted to the Uesugi; later joined the Oda as an aide to Sassa Narimasa. Followed Narimasa to Higo and fought the local uprising. After Narimasa's death, a ronin.

@28094
Shiina Yasutane ?-1576

@28095
An Etchu local lord. Adopted Nagao Kagenao, a cousin of Uesugi Kenshin, and fought the Jinbo as an Uesugi ally. Later allied with the Takeda, was attacked by Kenshin and is said to have died in defeat.

@28096
Hatakeyama Yoshinori ?-1574

@28097
Yoshitsuna's heir. When his grandfather Yoshitsugu and father were driven from Noto, he was set up as head while still a child. Caught in his senior vassals' feuds, he was murdered by an unknown hand.

@28098
Cho Tsuratatsu 1546-1619

@28099
Tsugutsura's second son. After his father and brother Tsunatsura died, he left the priesthood to head the house. Submitting to Oda Nobunaga, he became lord of Fukumizu in Noto; later an aide to Maeda Toshiie.
