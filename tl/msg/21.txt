@21000
Shibata Shigeie 1546-1587

@21001
Uesugi vassal. Served Kagekatsu after Kenshin's death and won him the civil war. But unhappy with his reward, plotted with Oda Nobunaga and rebelled; killed himself after five years of fighting.

@21002
Hatakeyama Yoshitsuna ?-1593

@21003
Yoshitsugu's heir. With his father tried to regain real power as Constable of Noto, but was driven out by senior retainers and failed. Tried many times to re-enter Noto, but never succeeded.

@21004
Okudaira Nobumasa 1555-1615

@21005
Tokugawa vassal. Left Takeda Katsuyori to follow Ieyasu. At Nagashino held Nagashino Castle in Mikawa against the Takeda, helping win the battle. For this he married Ieyasu's daughter Princess Kame the next year.

@21006
Cho Tsugutsura ?-1577

@21007
Hatakeyama vassal; served four heads from Yoshitsugu on, one of the Hatakeyama Seven. Wielded vast power in league with Oda Nobunaga, but was slain by Uesugi Kenshin's army invading Noto.

@21008
Yusa Tsugumitsu ?-1581

@21009
Hatakeyama vassal. Beaten by Nukui Fusasada, fled, returning after Fusasada's death. Between Uesugi Kenshin and Oda Nobunaga he kept switching sides; later captured and beheaded.

@21010
Honganji Kyonyo 1558-1614

@21011
Kennyo's eldest son. Fiery by nature, urged fighting to the end even after the peace with Oda Nobunaga. After his father's death fought his brother Junnyo for the abbacy and founded today's Higashi Honganji.

@21012
Shimozuma Raisho ?-1575

@21013
Honganji temple official. Led the Echizen Ikko rebels for the abbot Kennyo, harrying Oda Nobunaga. When Nobunaga set out to wipe out the rebels, was killed with Asakura Kagetake and others.

@21014
Shimozuma Rairen 1537-1626

@21015
Honganji temple official. In the Ishiyama War led the whole army for the abbot Kennyo, harrying Oda Nobunaga. After Nobunaga's death made Honganji town magistrate by Toyotomi Hideyoshi.

@21016
Okabe Motonobu ?-1581

@21017
Imagawa vassal. After Okehazama brought his lord Yoshimoto's head back to Suruga. After the Imagawa fell served the Takeda, holding Takatenjin Castle in Totomi; killed when the Tokugawa attacked it.

@21018
Sakuma Morimasa 1553-1583

@21019
Shibata vassal and Katsuie's nephew; a fierce warrior called "Demon Genba". At Shizugatake killed Nakagawa Kiyohide but pushed too deep, causing the Shibata defeat. Caught in the hills afterwards and beheaded.

@21020
Asahina Yasutomo ?-?

@21021
Took in his lord Ujizane, driven from Sunpu, at Kakegawa Castle in Totomi and fought the Tokugawa. Never yielding a step to the great host, fought fiercely for five months: the last stand of Imagawa pride.

@21022
Endo Naotsune ?-1570

@21023
Azai vassal. Urged his lord Nagamasa to assassinate Oda Nobunaga, in vain. At Anegawa tried to ride alone into Nobunaga's headquarters but failed and was killed.

@21024
Kaiho Tsunachika ?-1573

@21025
Azai vassal; father of the painter Kaiho Yusho. Served as master of arms; killed when Odani Castle fell. Toyotomi Hideyoshi is said to have later called him "my teacher in the art of war".

@21026
Isono Kazumasa ?-?

@21027
Azai vassal; lord of Sawayama Castle in Omi. Led the vanguard at Anegawa, harrying the Oda. Served the Oda after the Azai fell, but later angered Nobunaga and became a ronin.

@21028
Kutsuki Mototsuna 1549-1632

@21029
Azai vassal. Guided Oda Nobunaga in the retreat from Kanegasaki. After Honnoji followed Toyotomi Hideyoshi. At Sekigahara turned from the West to the East and was confirmed in his lands.

@21030
Asakura Kageakira ?-1574

@21031
A senior member of the Asakura kin. Plotted with Oda Nobunaga to drive the head Yoshikage to suicide, and was confirmed in his lands. Attacked by Ikko rebels, he was beaten and killed.

@21032
Magara Naotaka ?-1570

@21033
Asakura vassal, known with his brother Naozumi as one of the clan's mightiest warriors. At Anegawa, as his side fled, fought on against the Tokugawa and was killed with his son Takamoto.

@21034
Hatano Hideharu ?-1579

@21035
Lord of Yakami Castle in Tanba. Retook his castle from the Miyoshi and grew strong. Besieged by Akechi Mitsuhide, held out well, but after a year and a half was captured and executed.

@21036
Isshiki Yoshimichi ?-1579

@21037
Lord of Hatta Castle in Tango. Attacked by the Oda for sheltering the Shogun Ashikaga Yoshiaki after Oda Nobunaga drove him from Kyoto. Repelled them once, but lost through a traitor and killed himself.

@21038
Goto Katatoyo ?-1563

@21039
Rokkaku vassal and chief elder. With the Gamo and Ikeda drew up the rules of rewards. Often rebuked his hot-blooded lord Yoshiharu, who came to hate him and had him murdered.

@21040
Miyoshi Yoshitsugu 1551-1573

@21041
Son of Sogo Kazunaga. After Miyoshi Chokei died, became head of the main line under the Miyoshi Triumvirate. Submitted to Oda Nobunaga, but sheltered the exiled Ashikaga Yoshiaki; attacked and killed.

@21042
Yamana Toyokuni 1548-1626

@21043
Toyosada's third son. Retook Tottori Castle in Inaba from Takeda Takanobu and became Constable of Inaba. Later yielded to Hashiba Hideyoshi; driven from Tottori by his retainers, became Hideyoshi's storyteller.

@21044
Miyoshi Nagayuki ?-?

@21045
First of the Miyoshi Triumvirate. Served Yoshitsugu after Chokei's death but feuded with Matsunaga Hisahide, splitting the clan. Later beaten by Oda Nobunaga's army in the Kinai, he vanished.

@21046
Miyoshi Masayasu 1528-1615

@21047
One of the Miyoshi Triumvirate. Fled after defeat by Oda Nobunaga; later served the Toyotomi and died in the Osaka Summer Campaign. Said to be the model for Miyoshi Seikai of the Ten Braves of Sanada.

@21048
Kii Shigefusa 1536-1588

@21049
A local lord of Buzen who changed his name from Utsunomiya to Kii. Served the Ouchi, Otomo and Shimazu in turn to keep his lands. Yielded to Toyotomi Hideyoshi, but refused a move to Iyo, rebelled and was murdered.

@21050
Okubo Choan 1545-1613

@21051
Tokugawa vassal. Ran the finances of 1,200,000 koku of shogunal land, called "the Realm's Chief Intendant". Raised gold and silver output with new methods, laying the shogunate's economic base.

@21052
Atagi Fuyuyasu ?-1564

@21053
Miyoshi Motonaga's third son; skilled in poetry, calligraphy and tea. Heir of the Atagi, led the Awaji navy. Aided his brother Chokei well, but was killed by him when Chokei believed Matsunaga Hisahide's slander.

@21054
Suzuki Sadayu ?-1585

@21055
Head of the Saika band. With his army of gun-armed mercenaries, joined Honganji against Oda Nobunaga. Later fought Toyotomi Hideyoshi, lost, and was killed.

@21056
Mori Takamoto 1523-1563

@21057
Motonari's heir. Sent as a hostage to the Ouchi, came of age under Yoshitaka. With his father's guidance worked on the conquest of Chugoku, but died suddenly in Aki on the way to fight the Amago.

@21058
Shimizu Muneharu 1537-1582

@21059
Mori vassal; lord of Bitchu-Takamatsu Castle. Resisted Oda Nobunaga's western expedition, but was hard pressed by Hashiba Hideyoshi's flooding siege. Killed himself to make peace and spare his garrison.

@21060
Kuchiba Michiyoshi 1513-1582

@21061
Mori vassal. Under Kikkawa Motoharu handled warfare mainly in San'in. After Motonari's death became one of the Four, aiding the head Terumoto as a pillar of the Mori.

@21062
Fukuhara Sadatoshi 1512-1593

@21063
Mori vassal. Under Kobayakawa Takakage led the troops on the San'yo front. After Motonari's death became one of the Four, aiding the head Terumoto as a leader of the Mori.

@21064
Kikkawa Motonaga 1548-1587

@21065
Motoharu's eldest son, as brave as his father; in Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Kyushu campaign he is said to have won every fight. Took over when his father retired, but died of illness soon after his father's death.

@21066
Murakami Takeyoshi 1533-1604

@21067
Head of the Noshima Murakami navy. Sided with the Mori at Itsukushima and won them the battle. Thereafter served the Mori, fighting well everywhere, as in the great victory over the Oda navy at Kizugawaguchi.

@21068
Honda Shigetsugu 1529-1596

@21069
Tokugawa vassal; magistrate of Okazaki, called "Demon Sakuza". Angered Toyotomi Hideyoshi and was confined, said to be for treating Hideyoshi's mother coldly.

@21070
Mimura Iechika ?-1566

@21071
Lord of Bitchu-Matsuyama Castle. Subdued the local lords and became the leading power in Bitchu. Seeking to expand into Bizen and Mimasaka, took castles under Ukita Naoie, but was assassinated by Naoie.

@21072
Bessho Nagaharu 1558-1580

@21073
Lord of Miki Castle in Harima. Agreed to lead the vanguard of Oda Nobunaga's western expedition, but later turned against him. Besieged by Hashiba Hideyoshi for about two years, killed himself to spare his garrison.

@21074
Uragami Munekage ?-?

@21075
Muramune's son. Fighting his brother Masamune in Harima, expanded into Bizen. When his senior retainer Ukita Naoie rose to power, lost to him and was driven from Bizen.

@21076
Yamana Toyosada 1512-1560

@21077
Okitoyo's son. When his brother Suketoyo became Constable of Tajima, was ordered to hold down the Yamana of Inaba, who sought independence. Thereafter entrusted with the rule of Inaba.

@21078
Yamana Suketoyo 1511-1580

@21079
Okitoyo's son. Adopted by his uncle Nobutoyo, became Constable of Tajima. Sent his brother Toyosada to Inaba and ruled both Tajima and Inaba. Later attacked by Oda Nobunaga and surrendered.

@21080
Ukita Tadaie ?-1609

@21081
Ukita vassal. Did much to help his brother Naoie build the house. After Naoie's death became guardian of his nephew Hideie. In Korea crossed the sea with Hideie, commander of the Toyotomi army.

@21082
Akashi Teruzumi ?-?

@21083
Ukita vassal. Became a ronin after Sekigahara and entered Osaka Castle, fighting hard at the head of Christian samurai. So great was his valour that the alarmed Tokugawa hunted for him after the war.

@21084
Shiga Chikatsugu 1566-?

@21085
Otomo vassal. Held Oka Castle in Bungo with only 1,000 men against 35,000 under Niiro Tadamoto of the Shimazu. Broke the siege and retook castles nearby, earning Toyotomi Hideyoshi's praise for his valour.

@21086
Ashina Moritaka 1561-1584

@21087
Son of Nikaido Moriyoshi. Married Morioki's widow and became 18th head of the Ashina. Fought the Satake in league with the Uesugi, Takeda and Hojo. Killed in Kurokawa Castle by an aide resentful at losing favour.

@21088
Oda Ujiharu 1531-1601

@21089
Lord of Oda Castle in Hitachi. Allied with the Hojo to stop the Satake push south, but lost battle after battle, lost his castle and surrendered. Never regained his lands; served Yuki Hideyasu.

@21090
Soma Moritane 1529-1601

@21091
Akitane's heir. At deadly war with the Date ever since the Tenbun Rebellion. Meanwhile made peace with his neighbour Tamura Kiyoaki, securing his domain.

@21092
Yoshimi Masayori 1513-1588

@21093
Lord of Tsuwano Castle in Iwami. Served the Ouchi and married Yoshitaka's daughter. When Sue Harukata rebelled, joined Mori Motonari to overthrow him. Served the Mori after Itsukushima.

@21094
Sogo Kazunaga ?-1561

@21095
Miyoshi Motonaga's fourth son. Heir of the Sogo, lord of Sogo Castle in Sanuki. Swept the Kinai with the Sanuki men, feared as "Demon Sogo". Died falling from his horse on a pilgrimage to the Arima shrine.

@21096
Chosokabe Nobuchika 1565-1586

@21097
Motochika's heir. Given a character of Oda Nobunaga's name, he took the name Nobuchika. Brave and well loved; killed at Hetsugigawa in Bungo. Motochika's grief is said to have been terrible.

@21098
Kira Chikazane 1563-1588

@21099
Chikasada's heir. In the Chosokabe succession, rebuked Motochika for wanting his fourth son Morichika as heir. For this Motochika, believing the slander of his rival Hisatake Chikanao, made him kill himself.
