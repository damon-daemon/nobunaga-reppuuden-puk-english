A Mimura vassal. Opposed his nephew Motochika's plan to turn to Oda Nobunaga to regain their lands, and was banished. Later guided the Mori army sent against Motochika and became lord of Tsurukubi Castle in Bitchu.

@31002
Ishikawa Hisatomo ?-1567

@31003
A local lord holding half of Bitchu, based at Takayama Castle in Bizen. Sided with Mimura Motochika at the Battle of Myozenji, but wounded in defeat by Ukita Naoie, he took his own life.

@31004
Ishikawa Hisanori ?-1575

@31005
Hisatomo's son; inherited on his father's death. When the Amago took his seat, he won it back with Mori help. Later, when the Mimura and Mori fell out, he sided with the Mimura and was killed by the Mori.

@31006
Sugihara Morishige ?-1581

@31007
A Mori vassal. Served Sugihara Tadaoki, lord of Kannabe Castle in Bingo; after Tadaoki's death, became its keeper on Kikkawa Motoharu's recommendation. Then followed Motoharu, leading the vanguard in San'in.

@31008
Hoida Motokiyo 1551-1597

@31009
Mori Motonari's fourth son; succeeded to the Hoida, a Bitchu local house. Won merit in many places, taking Kozuki Castle in Harima, held by Yamanaka Shikanosuke. Later took back the Mori name.

@31010
Mori Hidemoto 1579-1650

@31011
Son of Hoida Motokiyo, adopted by his cousin Terumoto. In Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Korean campaign he led the Mori army in Terumoto's place. When Terumoto had a son of his own, he founded a branch house.

@31012
Kumagai Nobunao 1507-1593

@31013
A Mori vassal. First Motonari's enemy, later his vassal. After his daughter married Motonari's second son Kikkawa Motoharu, he was trusted as kin and fought hard in many places in the Kikkawa vanguard.

@31014
Kumagai Motonao 1555-1605

@31015
Nobunao's grandson. Served the Mori in many battles. Under Kuroda Kanbei's influence he became a Christian. Later, refusing to renounce his faith, he was put to death with his whole family.

@31016
Shishido Takaie 1518-1592

@31017
A Mori vassal and Aki local lord, married to a daughter of Motonari. Trusted as kin alongside Kobayakawa Takakage and Kikkawa Motoharu, he helped the Mori expand.

@31018
Kodama Narikata 1513-1586

@31019
A Mori vassal; recommended by his brother Mototada, he served Motonari. When the Sue were driven from Aki, he became lord of Kusatsu Castle in Aki. Led the Mori navy at sea off north Kyushu and San'in.

@31020
Amano Takashige 1503-1584

@31021
An Ouchi vassal who served the Mori after the Ouchi fell. After the Amago's fall, became keeper of Gassan-Toda Castle in Izumo, and later repulsed Yamanaka Shikanosuke's army when it attacked the castle.

@31022
Ichikawa Tsuneyoshi ?-?

@31023
A Mori vassal. Arranged for Motonari's second son Motoharu to succeed to the Kikkawa. After the Ouchi fell he held Konomine Castle in Suo; as Yamaguchi magistrate he ran the affairs of Suo and Nagato.

@31024
Hiraga Motosuke 1547-1645

@31025
A Mori vassal. A favourite of Toyotomi Hideyoshi, who granted him the Toyotomi name. Held Takaya in Aki, 18,000 koku; when the Mori lands were cut after Sekigahara, he retired and went to Kyoto.

@31026
Nomi Munekatsu 1527-1592

@31027
A Mori vassal. Though an elder of Kobayakawa Takakage, he also commanded the Mori navy. At Itsukushima he won over the Murakami navy, related by marriage, and led the Mori to victory.

@31028
Katada Motoyoshi 1568-1622

@31029
A Mori vassal, born an Awaya; his lord Terumoto granted him the Katada name. Became lord of Mihara Castle in Bingo after Kobayakawa Takakage was moved to Iyo. Went to Sekigahara in Terumoto's place.

@31030
Miura Mototada 1555-1596

@31031
A Mori vassal, born a Kanda. Married the daughter of Shigesawa Motouji, Kikkawa Motoharu's second son, and took over the Miura house Motouji had inherited. Served Terumoto as a close aide and go-between.

@31032
Reizei Takatoyo 1513-1551

@31033
An Ouchi vassal; lord of Kanayama Castle in Aki. When Sue Harukata rebelled, he fled to Nagato with his lord Yoshitaka and was his second at his suicide. Then fought the Sue and died a terrible death.

@31034
Hironaka Takakane ?-1555

@31035
An Ouchi vassal and Deputy Constable of Aki; Yoshinaga's right hand after Yoshitaka's death. Tricked by Mori Motonari, he killed Era Fusahide; died in the Mori surprise attack at Itsukushima.

@31036
Naito Takaharu 1528-1600

@31037
An Ouchi vassal. After the Ouchi fell he served the Mori, his sister being Mori Takamoto's wife, and kept order in Suo and Nagato. Later went to Osaka and reported on affairs in the capital.

@31038
Sugi Shigenori ?-1551

@31039
An Ouchi vassal and Deputy Constable of Buzen. Conspired with his old rival Sue Harukata in revolt and drove his lord Yoshitaka to suicide. Later turned against Harukata again and killed himself in Nagato.

@31040
Sugi Shigeyoshi 1553-1579

@31041
An Ouchi vassal; Shigenori's grandson. After the Ouchi fell he fought Sue Harukata, and after Itsukushima served Mori Motonari. Later went over to the Otomo; said to have been killed by the rebel Takahashi Kanetane.

@31042
Sugi Okikazu ?-1551

@31043
An Ouchi vassal and Deputy Constable of Chikuzen. Led the army at Tade-nawate in Hizen, but lost to Ryuzoji Iekane of the Shoni. When Sue Harukata rebelled, he died with his lord Yoshitaka.

@31044
Kawamura Shigeyoshi 1575-1648

@31045
A Date vassal, expert in mathematics and waterworks. Ran many public works, starting with the improvement of the Kitakami River; laid the economic base of Sendai domain, said to be really worth 2 million koku.

@31046
Hosokawa Mochitaka ?-1553

@31047
Head of the Awa Hosokawa; inherited on his father Yukimochi's death as Constable of Awa. Planned to enter Kyoto with Yoshihide, grandson of Ashikaga Yoshizumi, but fell out with his vassal Miyoshi Yoshikata and was killed.

@31048
Hosokawa Saneyuki ?-1582

@31049
Mochitaka's son; inherited on his father's death. Set up by his father's killer Miyoshi Yoshikata as lord of Shozui Castle in Awa, but he was a mere puppet: real power lay with the Miyoshi.

@31050
Atagi Nobuyasu ?-1578

@31051
Fuyuyasu's son; inherited on his father's death and led the Awaji navy. Allied with the Ishiyama Honganji, but later submitted to Oda Nobunaga's army and fought the Mori navy at Kizugawaguchi.

@31052
Shinohara Nagafusa ?-1573

@31053
A Miyoshi vassal. Enacted the "Shinka Seishiki", a provincial law code. Ranked just below the Miyoshi Triumvirate, but slandered by his kinsman Shinohara Jiton, he was killed by his lord Nagaharu.

@31054
Kagawa Motokage ?-?

@31055
A Hosokawa vassal who followed the Miyoshi after his lord's fall. Made friendly overtures to Oda Nobunaga in Kyoto, but submitted to Chosokabe Motochika. Retired, leaving the house to Motochika's second son Chikakazu.

@31056
Kono Michinao ?-1572

@31057
Head of the Kono, seated at Yuzuki Castle in Iyo. His plan to leave the house to his son-in-law Kurushima Michiyasu set his vassals feuding. In the end he made Michimasa of a branch line heir and became a monk.

@31058
Tokui Michitoshi ?-1597

@31059
Son of Kurushima Michiyasu. Descended from the Doi and Tokuno, he took one character from each to form the name Tokui. Went on Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Korean campaign with his brother Kurushima Michifusa and died in battle.

@31060
Kurushima Michifusa 1561-1597

@31061
Fourth son of Kurushima Michiyasu. Served the Kono, but went over to Hashiba Hideyoshi's army in the west. Then followed Hideyoshi as a naval commander; died in the Korean campaign.

@31062
Kurushima Nagachika 1582-1612

@31063
Michifusa's son; inherited on his father's death. Went from the West to the East at Sekigahara and was given Mori in Bungo, 13,000 koku. It lay inland, however, leaving his navy nothing to do.

@31064
Murakami Yoshimitsu ?-?

@31065
A Mori vassal; chief of the Innoshima Murakami navy. A master of fire arrows, he shone at Itsukushima and in the sea battle with Nobunaga at Kizugawaguchi, Osaka, making the Murakami navy famous.

@31066
Saionji Sanemitsu ?-?

@31067
Lord of Kurose Castle in Iyo, holding the Uwa area of south Iyo. His son Kintaka died in a border war with the Utsunomiya, so he adopted his nephew Kinhiro. Skilled in arts and arms; went to Kyoto for poetry gatherings.

@31068
Doi Seiryo 1546-1629

@31069
Lord of Omori Castle in Iyo; one of the Fifteen Saionji Generals. Fled to Tosa when his grandfather Kiyomune died in battle; regained his lands with the Tosa Ichijo's help. The "Seiryoki" praises his wits and valour.

@31070
Kagawa Chikakazu ?-1587

@31071
Chosokabe Motochika's second son. Became head of the Kagawa at Amagiri Castle in Sanuki; beaten by Toyotomi Hideyoshi, he went back to Tosa. Passed over as heir when his brother Nobuchika died; died of illness in despair.

@31072
Yoshida Shigetoshi ?-?

@31073
Yoshida Takayori's brother; a shrewd warrior who helped his brother raise the Chosokabe. Above all, against Aki Kunitora he made the Aki house collapse from within, opening the way to victory.

@31074
Yoshida Masashige 1568-1628

@31075
A Chosokabe vassal; a fierce warrior said to have taken 115 heads and suffered 21 wounds in his life. Fought hard at Osaka under his old lord Morichika. After the war he went back to Tosa and became a doctor.

@31076
Yoshida Yasutoshi 1565-1634

@31077
A Chosokabe vassal; Shigetoshi's great-grandson. Served Motochika and Morichika, fighting hard at Nakatomigawa and Hetsugigawa. Briefly a Yamanouchi vassal, he joined Morichika on the Osaka side in the Osaka campaigns.

@31078
Hisatake Chikanobu ?-1579

@31079
A Chosokabe vassal; honest and sensible, deeply trusted by Motochika. Charged with conquering south Iyo, he fought the local lords again and again. Died in battle attacking Okamoto Castle in Iyo.

@31080
Tani Tadazumi 1534-1600

@31081
A Chosokabe vassal in charge of diplomacy. When Toyotomi Hideyoshi invaded Shikoku, he told his lord Motochika resistance was folly. He arranged peace and saved the house from ruin.

@31082
Fukutome Norishige 1549-1586

@31083
A Chosokabe vassal; a warrior sung of even in children's songs. Helped Motochika unify Shikoku. When a ban on drink was issued, he smashed a sake barrel in protest. Died at Hetsugigawa in Bungo.

@31084
Kuwana Yoshinari ?-1615

@31085
A Chosokabe vassal; distinguished himself at Hetsugigawa and elsewhere. After Sekigahara he served Todo Takatora. In the summer siege of Osaka he fought his old lord Chosokabe Morichika and died in battle.

@31086
Motoyama Shigemune ?-?

@31087
A Tosa local lord based at Motoyama Castle. Fought the Chosokabe, who aimed to unify Tosa. Later left Motoyama Castle to his son Shigetatsu and moved to Asakura Castle in Tosa.

@31088
Motoyama Shigetatsu ?-?

@31089
Lord of Motoyama Castle in Tosa; Shigemune's heir. Married Chosokabe Kunichika's eldest daughter, but the feud went on; beaten when Kunichika attacked him. His life was spared, but his house fell under Motochika.

@31090
Aki Kunitora ?-1569

@31091
Lord of Aki Castle in Tosa. Struck first at the rising Chosokabe, attacking Oko Castle with Ichijo help, but was beaten. Later attacked by Chosokabe Motochika, he took his own life.

@31092
Ichijo Fusamoto 1522-1549

@31093
Head of the Tosa Ichijo, descended from the Fujiwara. Inherited on his father Fusafuyu's death and received high court rank. Seated at the Nakamura Palace in Tosa, he fought local clans; killed himself for unknown reasons.

@31094
Ojima Masaaki ?-1591

@31095
An Ichijo vassal. When his lord Kanesada was banished, he angrily attacked the seats of the elders who had betrayed him. Submitted to Chosokabe Motochika's invasion and worked to pacify the Hata district.

@31096
Kii Nagafusa 1506-1588

@31097
15th head of the Kii Utsunomiya of Buzen. Left the house young to his son Shigefusa, trusting him to restore it. Submitted to Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Kyushu campaign, but later fell out with Kuroda Nagamasa and was murdered.

@31098
Otomo Yoshiaki 1502-1550

@31099
20th head of the Otomo. Fought the Ouchi and others and took Bungo, Chikugo and Higo. His plan to disinherit his eldest son Yoshishige set off the Nikai Kuzure Incident; cut down by his vassals, he died.
