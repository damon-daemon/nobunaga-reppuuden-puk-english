@25000
Asano Nagaakira 1586-1632

@25001
Second son of Nagamasa. After his brother Yoshinaga died, he inherited Wakayama, Kii (370,000 koku). In the summer campaign at Osaka he slew Ban Danemon. After Fukushima Masanori was attainted, he held Hiroshima, Aki (420,000).

@25002
Takagi Taneyoshi ?-1565

@25003
Head of the Takagi; father of Tanetoki. The Takagi were first stewards of the Chiba of Shimosa, then senior vassals of the Hara. When the Hara moved their seat, he spent 8 years building Kogane Castle as his own.

@25004
Takagi Tanetoki ?-1582

@25005
Head of the Takagi; son of Taneyoshi. Sided with the Hojo and won the second battle of Konodai. Also helped the Hara stop Uesugi Kenshin's invasion, securing his place as a local lord.

@25006
Satomi Tadayoshi 1594-1622

@25007
Heir of Yoshiyasu; married a granddaughter of Okubo Tadachika. When Tadachika fell, he was exiled to Kurayoshi, Hoki. "The Eight Dog Chronicles" is said to be inspired by 8 retainers who died following him.

@25008
Ono Tadaaki ?-1628

@25009
Swordsman, first named Mikogami Tenzen. After serving Satomi Yoshiyasu he travelled the land to train, studied under Ito Ittosai and founded the Ono Itto school. Attainted at Sekigahara for breaking army rules.

@25010
Masaki Tokitada ?-1576

@25011
Satomi vassal who won merit in many battles. After the second battle of Konodai he defected to the Hojo to preserve the Masaki house, but in his last years was allowed to return.

@25012
Saito Toshimitsu 1534-1582

@25013
Saito vassal. Later served Akechi Mitsuhide and took part in the Honnoji Incident. Beaten at Yamazaki, he was beheaded in Kyoto. Father of Lady Kasuga, wet nurse of the 3rd Tokugawa shogun Iemitsu.

@25014
Masaki Yoritada 1551-1622

@25015
Sent as hostage when his father Tokitada submitted to the Hojo; returned to Katsura Castle, Kazusa, after his father died. When the Satomi were attainted, he went to Kii to his son Tameharu, a Kii Tokugawa vassal.

@25016
Masaki Tameharu 1573-1652

@25017
Heir of Yoritada; served Tokugawa Ieyasu. When Yorinobu, son of Ieyasu and his sister Oman, was moved to Wakayama in Kii, he moved there too and became an elder of the Kii Tokugawa.

@25018
Utsunomiya Hisatsuna 1512-1549

@25019
Head of the Utsunomiya of Shimotsuke; father of Hirotsuna. Backed Nasu Masasuke against his son Takasuke, with the Satake and Oda. Then attacked Takasuke at Kitsuregawa; routed and slain.

@25020
Haga Takatsugu ?-1592

@25021
Second son of Takatsune. Senior Elder to two Utsunomiya heads, Hirotsuna and Kunitsuna, running the household. Worked hard to strengthen alliances with the Satake and Yuki against the Hojo offensive.

@25022
Haga Takatake 1572-1612

@25023
Third son of Utsunomiya Hirotsuna. Took over the Haga, the clan's senior elders, and was the right hand of his brother Kunitsuna. After the Utsunomiya were attainted, he often offered prayers at Ise for their revival.

@25024
Mibu Tsunao ?-1576

@25025
Lord of Mibu Castle, Shimotsuke. Drew close to the Hojo to break free of the Utsunomiya. Later his uncle, in league with the Utsunomiya, invited him to Kanuma Castle and murdered him.

@25026
Mibu Yoshio ?-1590

@25027
Son of Tsunao; held Mibu and Kanuma Castles in Shimotsuke. An Utsunomiya vassal, he later served the Hojo. His lands were confirmed after Odawara, but with no son his line died out.

@25028
Nasu Takasuke ?-1551

@25029
Head of the Nasu; lord of Karasuyama Castle, Shimotsuke. Clashed with his father Masasuke; repelled Utsunomiya Hisatsuna and others from Karasuyama. Later killed by a plot of Utsunomiya Hirotsuna and Haga Takasada.

@25030
Kuki Moritaka 1573-1632

@25031
Son of Yoshitaka. At Sekigahara he sided with the East, fighting his father, who sided with the West. He traded his merit for his father's life and was granted it, but his father had already killed himself.

@25032
Nasu Suketane ?-1583

@25033
Second son of Masasuke; lord of Karasuyama Castle, Shimotsuke. Succeeded after his brother Takasuke died. Fought neighbouring lords again and again to expand into north Kanto, and united Upper and Lower Nasu.

@25034
Sano Masatsuna ?-1574

@25035
Lord of Karasawayama Castle, Shimotsuke; followed Ashikaga Yoshiuji, the Koga kubo. Attacked many times by Uesugi Kenshin, he held the castle with help from the Hojo and Takeda.

@25036
Sano Munetsuna ?-1585

@25037
Lord of Karasawayama Castle, Shimotsuke; thought to be Masatsuna's eldest son. Allied with the Satake to fight the Hojo, and also feuded with the Nagao of Tatebayashi; died in battle at Hikoma.

@25038
Sano Fusatsuna 1558-1601

@25039
Lord of Karasawayama Castle, Shimotsuke. When Hojo Ujitada seized the Sano headship, he left to serve Hideyoshi. Known as "Tentokuji Ryohaku", he is said to have been a master swordsman.

@25040
Minagawa Hiroteru 1548-1627

@25041
Lord of Minagawa Castle, Shimotsuke. Surrendered to Tokugawa Ieyasu during Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Odawara campaign. Later elder to Ieyasu's sixth son Matsudaira Tadateru; attainted for reporting his misdeeds.

@25042
Nagao Norinaga 1503-1550

@25043
Of the Ashikaga Nagao; father of Kagenaga. Aided Kanto Kanrei Uesugi Norihiro. When Koga kubo Ashikaga Takamoto's son came of age, shogun Ashikaga Yoshiharu granted him a character of his name.

@25044
Nagao Kagenaga 1527-1569

@25045
Of the Ashikaga Nagao. First named "Masanaga" after a character granted by Uesugi Norimasa. After the Echigo-Sagami peace, he worked with Yura Narishige as go-between for the Uesugi and Hojo.

@25046
Nagao Akinaga ?-1621

@25047
Second son of Yura Narishige. Married Nagao Kagenaga's daughter and took over the Ashikaga Nagao. Fought for the Hojo against Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Odawara campaign; after the Hojo fell, he became a wanderer.

@25048
Nagano Narimori 1546-1563

@25049
Yamanouchi-Uesugi vassal. Became lord of Minowa Castle, Kozuke, on his father Narimasa's death. Under fierce attack by the Takeda, who sought Kozuke, he could not hold and took his life, aged only 18.

@25050
Yura Narishige 1506-1578

@25051
Lord of Kanayama Castle at Nitta, Kozuke. Joined Uesugi Kenshin's Kanto campaigns against the Hojo and Takeda, but later submitted to the Hojo and worked on their alliance talks with the Uesugi.

@25052
Yura Kunishige 1550-1611

@25053
Heir of Narishige. Held out in Odawara Castle against Toyotomi Hideyoshi's siege. His mother and son Sadashige won merit on the Toyotomi side, so after the Hojo fell he got lands at Ushiku, Hitachi.

@25054
Numata Akiyasu ?-?

@25055
Lord of Numata Castle, Kozuke, which he built. Submitted to Uesugi Kenshin when he invaded Kanto. Later killed his heir Tomonori to favour his youngest son Kageyoshi; the Numata men rose and he fled.

@25056
Obata Nobusada 1540-1592

@25057
Takeda vassal; a mainstay of the West Kozuke vanguard. After the Takeda fell he served the Takigawa and Hojo, and surrendered to Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Odawara campaign. Afterwards protected by Sanada Masayuki.

@25058
Uesugi Tomosada 1525-1546

@25059
Last head of the Ogigayatsu-Uesugi. Advanced on Odawara but was driven back by Hojo Ujitsuna and lost Kawagoe Castle. Later besieged Kawagoe, but was routed by Hojo Ujiyasu's surprise attack and fell.

@25060
Nanbada Yoshikane ?-1546

@25061
Ogigayatsu-Uesugi vassal. In the siege of Kawagoe, he rallied the badly outmatched Uesugi troops. Fighting all over the field in the night battle, he fell into an old well and died.

@25062
Ueda Tomonao 1494-1582

@25063
Former Ogigayatsu-Uesugi vassal; surrendered to Hojo Ujiyasu's invasion of Musashi and served him. As lord of Matsuyama Castle, Musashi, he used his own seal and ruled independently of the Hojo.

@25064
Ueda Norisada ?-?

@25065
Second son of Tomonao. Like his father and brother Naganori he served the Hojo and developed the town below Matsuyama Castle, Musashi. Held out in Odawara Castle in the siege; vanished after it fell.

@25066
Kiso Yoshiyasu 1514-1579

@25067
Landholder based in the Kiso valley, Shinano; with the Murakami, Ogasawara and Suwa, one of the "Four Generals of Shinano". Beaten by Takeda Shingen's repeated attacks, he submitted.

@25068
Ota Yasusuke 1531-1581

@25069
Son of Suketaka. An Ogigayatsu-Uesugi vassal, he went over to the Hojo, but discontent with his treatment made him ally with the Satomi. Beaten by Hojo Ujiyasu's army at Konodai.

@25070
Narita Osatada ?-1616

@25071
Younger brother of Ujinaga. Served the Hojo, and after their fall became a Gamo Ujisato vassal with his brother. After his brother's death he inherited Karasuyama, Shimotsuke (37,000 koku). Fought at Osaka too.

@25072
Hojo Ujikatsu 1559-1611

@25073
Son of Ujishige; became lord of Tamanawa Castle, Sagami, on his father's death. In Toyotomi Hideyoshi's siege of Odawara he held Yamanaka Castle, Izu, then Tamanawa. After surrendering he served Tokugawa Ieyasu.

@25074
Toyama Tsunakage ?-1564

@25075
Hojo vassal; chief of the Edo band. Records say he invited the linked-verse master Soboku to Edo Castle for a poetry gathering. Led the van at the second battle of Konodai and fell fighting in vain.

@25076
Ogasawara Sadayoshi 1546-1595

@25077
Heir of Nagatoki. Beaten by Takeda Shingen, he wandered the land; with Tokugawa Ieyasu's backing he regained his old lands. Briefly sided with Toyotomi Hideyoshi; after Komaki-Nagakute, returned to Ieyasu.

@25078
Kojima Motoshige ?-?

@25079
Jinbo vassal; lord of Hinomiya Castle, Etchu. Surrendered his castle to an Ikko uprising. Followed the Oda when they invaded Etchu. His revolt failed and he was destroyed by Shibata Katsuie and others.

@25080
Daidoji Naomasa 1569-1628

@25081
Thought to be Masashige's third son. After the Hojo fell he served the Maeda and the Owari Tokugawa. In the Korean campaign of the Bunroku era he fought bravely at Suncheon under Terasawa Hirotaka.

@25082
Hatakeyama Yoshitsugu ?-1590

@25083
Heir of Yoshifusa. Lost power through repeated revolts, letting senior vassals rise. Briefly regained control with his son Yoshitsuna, but the senior vassals turned on them and drove them from Noto.

@25084
Haga Yasutada ?-?

@25085
Hojo vassal; son of Ujitsugu. A close aide, granted the character "Yasu" by Hojo Ujiyasu. As magistrate and councillor he did much for Hojo rule in East Kozuke.

@25086
Shimizu Yasuhide ?-1591

@25087
Hojo vassal; chief of the Izu band. Raised in Ujiyasu's service, he won merit in many battles. In Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Odawara campaign he held Shimoda Castle, Izu, led the Izu navy in its defence, and lost.

@25088
Inomata Kuninori ?-1590

@25089
Hojo vassal. On his own authority he seized Nagurumi Castle, Kozuke, a branch castle of Sanada Masayuki; this gave Toyotomi Hideyoshi his pretext for Odawara. Crucified after Odawara Castle fell.

@25090
Kajiwara Kagemune ?-?

@25091
Hojo vassal. Invited from Kii by Ujiyasu to lead his navy; fought the Satomi and others. After Odawara he went with Ujinao to Mt. Koya; after Ujinao died, he returned to Kii.

@25092
Asahina Nobuoki 1528-1582

@25093
Imagawa vassal who won merit at Azukizaka and elsewhere. After the Imagawa fell he served Takeda Shingen in the Suruga vanguard. Beaten by the Tokugawa invading Suruga; killed himself on Oda Nobunaga's order.

@25094
Abe Motozane 1513-1587

@25095
Imagawa vassal. Held Sunpu Castle with Okabe Masatsuna, but when Takeda Shingen invaded Suruga he parted from Masatsuna and turned to Tokugawa Ieyasu. Spent his whole life fighting the Takeda.

@25096
Ii Naochika ?-1562

@25097
Imagawa vassal; lord of Iinoya Castle, Totomi, and father of Naomasa. Succeeded Naomori, killed at Okehazama, but his lord Ujizane suspected him of treason and had him killed.

@25098
Iio Tsuratatsu ?-1565

@25099
Imagawa vassal; lord of Hikuma Castle, Totomi. Served Yoshimoto, but when Yoshimoto fell at Okehazama, he went over to Tokugawa Ieyasu; for this his lord Ujizane had him murdered.
