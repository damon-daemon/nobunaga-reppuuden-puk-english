@17000
Ashikaga Yoshiaki 1537-1597

@17001
15th Muromachi Shogun. Resenting being made Oda Nobunaga's puppet, joined other daimyo to form a league against him. Took up arms himself but was beaten and driven from Kyoto.

@17002
Fukushima Masanori 1561-1624

@17003
Toyotomi vassal; Hideyoshi's cousin and first of the Seven Spears of Shizugatake. Led the Eastern Army's main force at Sekigahara and won 490,000 koku at Hiroshima in Aki. Later ruined by shogunate intrigue.

@17004
Kato Kiyomasa 1562-1611

@17005
Toyotomi vassal; one of the Seven Spears of Shizugatake. Shone in the Korean campaigns, earning the tale of "Tiger Kato". At odds with Ishida Mitsunari, he joined the East at Sekigahara and won 520,000 koku at Kumamoto.

@17006
Otani Yoshitsugu 1559-1600

@17007
Toyotomi vassal; sided with the West at Sekigahara. Fought on despite illness for his friend Ishida Mitsunari and routed Todo Takatora, but was struck from behind by the turncoat Kobayakawa Hideaki; killed himself.

@17008
Shima Katsutake ?-1600

@17009
Served the Tsutsui, then became a ronin; hired by Ishida Mitsunari for a huge stipend. Praised as "too good for Mitsunari", he fought everywhere at Sekigahara and died a glorious death.

@17010
Takenaka Hanbee 1544-1579

@17011
Saito vassal. Won over by Hashiba Hideyoshi's repeated humble visits, became his strategist and supported him from behind with brilliant schemes. Died suddenly during the Chugoku campaign, mourned by all.

@17012
Ii Naomasa 1561-1602

@17013
One of Tokugawa's Four Heavenly Kings. His troops, all clad in red, were feared as "Ii's Red Devils" and always vied for the vanguard. Pursued the Shimazu at Sekigahara and slew Shimazu Toyohisa.

@17014
Sakakibara Yasumasa 1548-1606

@17015
One of Tokugawa's Four Heavenly Kings. Raced across battlefields under a banner reading "Nothing", winning glory everywhere. Offered a senior council post late in life, refused: "old retainers seeking power ruin a realm".

@17016
Katakura Kagetsuna 1557-1615

@17017
Date vassal. Made Masamune's tutor at 19, the brains behind him. Persuaded Masamune to join Toyotomi Hideyoshi's siege of Odawara, saving the clan's lands.

@17018
Date Shigezane 1568-1646

@17019
Sanemoto's son; the Date's fiercest warrior and Masamune's sword arm, said to be "bold and far-sighted". In old age told tales of the Oshu wars to the third Tokugawa Shogun, Iemitsu.

@17020
Tsugaru Tamenobu 1550-1607

@17021
Adopted by Oura Tamenori. Served the Nanbu, then broke away and unified Tsugaru over 17 years. Joined Toyotomi Hideyoshi's siege of Odawara and was confirmed in his Tsugaru lands.

@17022
Yamamoto Kansuke 1501-1561

@17023
Takeda vassal, versed in every art of war and peace; Shingen's strategist. At Kawanakajima proposed the "woodpecker" plan; when the enemy saw through it, he took the blame, charged into the melee and died.

@17024
Sanada Yukitaka 1513-1574

@17025
Takeda vassal. Took by a plot Toishi Castle in Shinano, which Shingen had twice failed to capture, and was praised as wiser than Shingen. Thereafter served as one of his advisers in many campaigns.

@17026
Taigen Sessai 1496-1555

@17027
Though a monk, served as Imagawa Yoshimoto's strategist. Led the vanguard when the Imagawa pushed into Mikawa and beat Oda Nobuhide. Helped forge the Kai-Sagami-Suruga triple alliance.

@17028
Asakura Soteki 1477-1555

@17029
Younger brother of Asakura Yoshikage's great-grandfather; served three generations of the Asakura. Spent his life at war, quelling the Kaga Ikko uprising and fighting the Rokkaku with the Azai.

@17030
Gamo Ujisato 1556-1595

@17031
Katahide's son. Oda Nobunaga prized his talent and gave him a daughter in marriage. Served Toyotomi Hideyoshi after Nobunaga, ruling 920,000 koku at Aizu in Mutsu. Hideyoshi is said to have feared his ability.

@17032
Suzuki Shigehide ?-?

@17033
Said to be Sadayu's son; known as Saika Magoichi. Led the Negoro and Saika gunners in Ishiyama Honganji, harrying the Oda. After the surrender is said to have served Nobunaga and Toyotomi Hideyoshi.

@17034
Goto Matabee 1560-1615

@17035
Kuroda vassal. Fought as a samurai general, but became a ronin when suspected of treason. Later invited by Toyotomi Hideyori into Osaka Castle; led the ronin bravely and died in the Summer Campaign.

@17036
Yamanaka Shikanosuke 1545-1578

@17037
Amago vassal. As a youth prayed to the crescent moon, "Grant me every hardship...". Tried to restore his clan with Amago Katsuhisa but lost to the Mori; killed while being taken to Aki.

@17038
Sue Harukata 1521-1555

@17039
Ouchi vassal, called "the finest general in the west". Drove his lord Yoshitaka to suicide to make the clan his puppet. Fell into Mori Motonari's trap; surprised at Itsukushima and killed.

@17040
Tachibana Dosetsu 1513-1585

@17041
Otomo vassal. Crippled by lightning when young, he led the Otomo vanguard from a palanquin and was called "Thunder Incarnate". His fame is said to have reached as far as Tokai.

@17042
Tachibana Muneshige 1567-1642

@17043
Takahashi Shoun's son; married Dosetsu's daughter and became his adopted heir. Sided with the West at Sekigahara and lost his lands; later served the Tokugawa through Kato Kiyomasa, ruling 110,000 koku at Yanagawa.

@17044
Takahashi Shoun 1548-1586

@17045
Otomo vassal; a fierce warrior who with Dosetsu, adoptive father of his son Tachibana Muneshige, formed the clan's twin pillars. Met 50,000 Shimazu with 700 men at Iwaya Castle and died taking many with him.

@17046
Nabeshima Naoshige 1538-1618

@17047
Ryuzoji vassal; wise and brave, he did much for his clan's rise. Entrusted with the rule of the domain by his lord Masaie, seized real power. Drew close to Tokugawa Ieyasu and won control of Hizen.

@17048
Hosokawa Fujitaka 1534-1610

@17049
Ashikaga vassal, later served Oda Nobunaga. A man of culture initiated in the Kokin Denju. Followed Toyotomi Hideyoshi after Honnoji. Joined the East at Sekigahara, preserving the Hosokawa.

@17050
Hosokawa Tadaoki 1563-1645

@17051
Fujitaka's heir. A fine tea master, one of Rikyu's Seven Disciples. Served Toyotomi Hideyoshi with distinction. Joined the East at Sekigahara, ruling 396,000 koku at Nakatsu in Buzen.

@17052
Yagyu Toshiyoshi 1579-1650

@17053
Given sole mastery by his grandfather Muneyoshi, became heir to Yagyu Shinkage-ryu. Served Kato Kiyomasa but became a ronin after killing a man. After wandering, became sword master to the Owari Tokugawa.

@17054
Hayashi Razan 1583-1657

@17055
Founder of the Hayashi Neo-Confucian school; served Tokugawa Ieyasu. Slandered Toyotomi Hideyori over the Hokoji bell, sparking the Osaka campaigns. Under Iemitsu helped govern and drafted official papers.

@17056
Doi Toshikatsu 1573-1644

@17057
Tokugawa vassal; son of Mizuno Nobumoto, though some say Ieyasu's natural son. Served the Shoguns Hidetada and Iemitsu as senior councillor and Great Elder, doing much to secure the Edo shogunate.

@17058
Sakai Tadayo 1572-1636

@17059
Tokugawa vassal; chief of staff in both Osaka campaigns. With Doi Toshikatsu and Aoyama Tadatoshi tutored the third Shogun Iemitsu and stood at the heart of the shogunate; also a senior councillor.

@17060
Aoyama Tadatoshi 1578-1643

@17061
Tokugawa vassal; tutored Iemitsu with Doi Toshikatsu and Sakai Tadayo. Stripped of his lands after angering Iemitsu, refused all calls to return and retired. His son Munetoshi became tutor to Ietsuna.

@17062
Nagoya Sanzaburo ?-1603

@17063
Gamo vassal, a man of rare beauty. First into the fray against Kunohe Masazane, hailed as "Nagoya Sanza, the first spear". Later served Mori Tadamasa; killed in a quarrel with a fellow retainer.

@17064
Mukai Masatsuna 1557-1625

@17065
Takeda vassal. Took over the house when his father Masashige fell to a Tokugawa attack. After the Takeda fell, served the Tokugawa as fleet magistrate, at the core of the Tokugawa navy.

@17066
Sanada Daisuke 1602-1615

@17067
Yukimura's son, born at Kudoyama in Kii where his father was exiled. Entered Osaka Castle with him and fought from the Sanada Maru. In the Summer Campaign died with Toyotomi Hideyori as the castle fell.

@17068
Obata Kagenori 1572-1663

@17069
Takeda vassal; a scholar of Koshu military science. After the Takeda fell became a page to Tokugawa Hidetada. Later left the Tokugawa for Osaka Castle, while passing its secrets to the Tokugawa army.

@17070
Sasaki Kojiro ?-1612

@17071
Also called Ganryu. After years of training devised the "Swallow Cut". Sought to serve Mashita Nagamori, but Toyotomi Hideyoshi would not allow it. Lost a duel with Miyamoto Musashi on Ganryu Island and died.

@17072
Miyamoto Musashi 1584-1645

@17073
A master swordsman who founded his own Niten Ichi-ryu style and boasted 66 duels unbeaten. In later years a guest of Hosokawa Tadatoshi, he enjoyed painting and Zen and wrote the Book of Five Rings.

@17074
Kakizaki Suehiro 1507-1595

@17075
Yoshihiro's son. Made peace with the Ainu, long at odds with his forebears, and set rules for trading ships to Ezo. By this policy secured his place as lord of Ezo.

@17076
Ishikawa Takanobu 1504-1571

@17077
Second son of Nanbu Masayasu. Aided his nephew Harumasa in government and war, helping the Nanbu grow. When Harumasa's son Harutsugu died suddenly, Takanobu's son Nobunao succeeded to the Nanbu.

@17078
Marume Nagayoshi 1540-1629

@17079
Sagara vassal. Studied the sword under Kamiizumi Nobutsuna and founded Taisha-ryu. Caused a defeat by the Shimazu and was disgraced; later returned as military instructor and also worked to clear new land.

@17080
Yamada Arinaga 1578-1668

@17081
Arinobu's heir; a wise and brave Shimazu elder. Distinguished himself crushing Ijuin Tadazane's revolt and at Sekigahara. Worked to develop industry and educate retainers.

@17082
Watanabe Moritsuna 1542-1620

@17083
Tokugawa vassal. Won glory on campaign after campaign, earning the name "Spear Hanzo". At Sekigahara served in the guard; later became an elder of the Owari Tokugawa, aiding their head Yoshinao.

@17084
Sarutobi Sasuke ?-?

@17085
One of the Ten Braves of Sanada; a fictional character and master of Koga ninjutsu. Said to have travelled the land with the monk Miyoshi Seikai as a spy, probing the Tokugawa's secrets.

@17086
Kirigakure Saizo ?-?

@17087
One of the Ten Braves of Sanada; a fictional character, said to be based on Kirigakure Shikaemon, a real ninja under Yukimura. Said to have sorely troubled the Tokugawa at Osaka with Iga ninjutsu.

@17088
Takigawa Kazumasu 1525-1586

@17089
Oda vassal; shone in the conquest of the Kitabatake of Ise and elsewhere. After Kai was subdued, became Kanto Kanrei. After Honnoji, routed by the Hojo, he fled home and died of illness in despair.

@17090
Sassa Narimasa 1539-1588

@17091
Oda vassal; took Hokuriku as an aide of Shibata Katsuie. After Honnoji opposed Hashiba Hideyoshi, then surrendered to him. Failed to govern his new fief of Higo and was ordered to kill himself.

@17092
Tsutsui Junkei 1549-1584

@17093
Lord of Tsutsui Castle in Yamato; head of the clan at 2 when his father Junsho died. Fought Matsunaga Hisahide, who was spreading into Yamato, and lost his castle. Later served Oda Nobunaga and ruled all Yamato.

@17094
Araki Murashige 1535-1586

@17095
Oda vassal; a tea master counted among Rikyu's Seven Disciples. Lord of Arioka Castle in Settsu. Joined Honganji and the Mori against Nobunaga; when attacked, fled, leaving his wife and children behind.

@17096
Kuki Yoshitaka 1542-1600

@17097
Oda vassal. After a heavy defeat at Kizugawaguchi built iron-clad great ships and crushed the Mori navy. Made a daimyo for it, he was called "the Pirate Daimyo".

@17098
Hashiba Hidenaga 1540-1591

@17099
Hideyoshi's younger brother and right hand, who helped him win the realm. Gentle and well liked, handled dealings with other daimyo. Died of illness before Hideyoshi, mourned by all the lords.
